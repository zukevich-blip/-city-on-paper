SUBJECT: Request — web thumbnails from 'Rome dans sa grandeur' (1870), records obj08003573 / obj08003574, for a non-commercial educational atlas

BODY:
Dear colleagues of the Photographic Collection,

I am developing, as a private individual, "City on Paper / Город на бумаге", an independent, non-commercial, educational digital museum, freely available on GitHub Pages (https://zukevich-blip.github.io/-city-on-paper/).

Our atlas (https://zukevich-blip.github.io/-city-on-paper/eras/) starts from a 1769 map, leads to individual towns, and then to views by Félix and Philippe Benoist and other artists, with then-and-now comparisons. Every work is credited and links directly to the holding institution's record.

Your online catalogue includes two volumes of "Rome dans sa grandeur" (1870), with lithographs by Félix and Philippe Benoist: records obj08003573 (vol. 2, Rome chrétienne) and obj08003574 (vol. 3, Rome moderne). We reference 40 plates from them (Santi Apostoli, Villa Borghese, Ariccia, Tivoli, Castel Gandolfo, Nemi and others; list below). The photographs carry an "In Copyright" statement, so we show links only. Would you allow:
1. web thumbnails (max. 1000 px) of the individual plates, with your credit line, the photo or negative number and a link to the record;
2. or links only, as now;
3. or tell us whether your publication-permission form applies to this kind of web use.

Our commitments: credit and link to your record; rights statement shown; the original is never altered (no cropping, recolouring or AI); no commercial use; removal within 7 days on request.

For the Hertziana: visitors directed to your Online Catalogue, and Benoist's Rome placed on a map plate by plate.

A one-line reply is enough:
— "Yes, with credit line: …"
— "Links only, please"
— "Please contact: …"
