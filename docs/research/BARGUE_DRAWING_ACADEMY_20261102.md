# Школа художника · Bargue / Gérôme

Independent curatorial teaching chapter prompted by owner upload of the 2020 Russian edition of Charles Bargue and Jean-Léon Gérôme's **Cours de dessin**. **No uploaded-book scans, modern commentary or copyrighted translation have been committed**.

## What is directly supported by the uploaded 2020 book

- Full 2020 bibliographical attribution: Charles Bargue; Jean-Léon Gérôme; editor Gerald M. Ackerman; translator L. I. Stepanova; AST, Moscow, 384 printed pages; ISBN 978-5-17-118142-0. Original historical course developed from 1867 onward.
- The book, printed page 10, says the historical course has **197 separate lithographic plates** arranged progressively.
- Printed pp. 25–31 set out a practical process: outer extreme points, plumb lines/horizontals, straight-segment reduction of curved contours, shadow masses, mid-tones and checking/correction.
- Printed p. 32 / plate I,1 and p. 36 / plate I,5 show original study examples; in the provided scan PDF these are physical pages 33 and 37.
- Structurally: I, plaster-cast studies; II, masters' drawings; III, preparation for academic studies from life.
- The historical notes disagree in plate counts by part (70 and later 60, 60 = 190 vs total 197). Store total separately and do not imply verified reconciliation of issue/copy count.

## Deliverable in this branch

- Responsive standalone `atelier/bargue/index.html`, with an **original, self-created architectural study** (classical portal) drawn as inline SVG and an independent drawing canvas. Supports mouse/touch/Apple Pencil pointer drawing, undo, clear with confirmation, five guided steps, guide toggle, browser-local saving and PNG export.
- Makes no claim that this architectural example is a historical Bargue lithograph. It's an original exercise **applying** selected methods from the source to a city architectural subject.
- Keep Benoist historical lithograph gallery separate from the Bargue educational chapter.
- Links to original BnF archive records rather than republishing the modern Russian textbook.
