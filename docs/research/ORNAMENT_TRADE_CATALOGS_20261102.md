## Source A · Bakewell & Mullins (1887)

**Designs of Architectural Ornaments**, fifth edition (No. 5 “D”), Salem, Ohio; New York: Williams Printing Co., **1887**. The catalogue illustrates sheet-zinc, brass and copper architectural ornaments, sculptural figures for civic buildings, gargoyles, cornices, capitals, architectural panels, trade item numbers and historic prices.

- Full source: https://archive.org/details/designsofarchite00bake
- Open Library: https://openlibrary.org/books/OL33153961M/Designs_of_architectural_ornaments
- Owner-provided scan: `designsofarchite00bake.pdf`, 200 PDF pages.
- High-potential plates: PDF p. 7 lion/anvil #2598; p. 10 Liberty lighting the World #2530; p. 18 Columbus #2529 (New Orleans exposition); p. 22 Atlas #2653; p. 32 griffins; p. 43 oak and laurel spandrel; p. 47 cornices; p. 73 German Romanesque capitals.
- Underlying 1887 US publication is public domain, but still check digitization/attribution and publishing jurisdiction per chosen asset.

## Source B · Decorators Supply Corp. (catalogue 124)

**Illustrated Catalogue of Period Ornaments Cast in Composition and Wood Fibre for Woodwork-Furniture**, Chicago. Distinct industrial process, mass-produced furniture and architectural trim with product codes and dimensions.

- Full source: https://archive.org/details/illustratedca00deco
- Wikimedia Commons reference: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Illustrated_catalogue_of_period_ornaments_cast_in_composition_and_wood_fibre_for_woodwork-furniture_(IA_illustratedca00deco).pdf
- Owner-provided scan: `illustratedca00deco.pdf`, 368 PDF pages.
- High-potential plates: PDF pp. 12, 27, 38, 110, 180, 230.
- **Date discrepancy:** same work catalogued as **1900** by Internet Archive/Open Library (https://openlibrary.org/books/OL24980579M/Illustrated_catalogue_of_period_ornaments_cast_in_composition_and_wood_fibre_for_woodwork-furniture), and a **1964** edition is separately catalogued (https://openlibrary.org/books/OL44947427M/Illustrated_catalogue_of_period_ornaments_cast_in_composition_and_wood_fibre_for_woodwork-furniture). Owner's scanned front page says **Chicago 8**, a postal zone designation introduced later in the twentieth century. Without verifying this exact copy, **do not assign a precise year**, call it a nineteenth-century publication, or automatically publish image previews.
